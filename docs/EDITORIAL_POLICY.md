# Editorial policy

## Inclusion and exclusion

Include official courses, workshops, webinars, programmes, examinations, recordings, and guides with direct educational relevance to rare diseases, medical genetics, genomics, biobanking, patient advocacy, clinical research, diagnostics, regulation, data sharing, or ethics. Exclude promotional material without education, unverifiable aggregators, search snippets, and social posts when an official page exists.

## Evidence priority

Prefer the current official resource page, programme or prospectus, registration/application page, structured data, provider calendar/feed, then clearly labelled official archives. Each factual change needs field-level evidence and a direct URL.

## Conflicts, duplicates, and archives

Conflicting official values remain unchanged with `source_conflict` and both sources documented until a reviewer decides. Canonical URL equality and title/provider/date similarity identify duplicates; editions are not merged silently. A timeout, challenge, TLS failure, rate limit, or single 404 is not archival evidence. Repeated confirmed 404/410 responses only propose archival.

## Translation and corrections

Original official titles and provider names are preserved. Machine-produced content is `draft`, never described as official or reviewed. Corrections name the record, field, values, source, short evidence, and reviewer decision.

## Human approval

Event, application, registration, access, price, eligibility, provider, title, delivery, language, certificate, cancellation, postponement, archive, deletion, merge, and new-resource changes require a person to approve the pull request. Production publication is also manual.
