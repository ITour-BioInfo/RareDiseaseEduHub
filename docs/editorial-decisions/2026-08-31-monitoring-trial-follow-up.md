## Rejected monitor noise

- EURORDIS/Open Academy application announcements and retrospective news pages are supporting editorial content, not distinct education resources.
- NHS “About us” is navigation/organisation information, not an education resource.
- A changed normalized page hash without a changed structured title or date is not field-level factual evidence.

## Operational implementation

- Full discovery runs weekly; near-term dated records remain on the daily priority scan.
- Morning and priority evidence use separate report files, branches and uniquely named workflow artifacts.
- Candidates beyond the per-run validation limit persist in the compact state branch.
- Repeatedly failing sources use bounded exponential backoff; manual record checks remain available.
- All proposals remain draft, evidence-backed and subject to human editorial approval. Monitoring never auto-merges, edits factual records or deploys.
